Privacy Policy

Introduction

At Rutherford Physiotherapy, located in Vaughan, we are committed to maintaining the confidentiality and security of our patients' personal and health information. This privacy policy outlines our practices and procedures for collecting, using, disclosing, and protecting your personal and health information.

Collection of Personal and Health Information

We collect personal and health information from our patients, including but not limited to:

  • ✓Name, address, and contact information
  • ✓Health history, including medical conditions and treatment
  • ✓Insurance and billing information
  • ✓Records of treatment and care provided

This information is collected directly from our patients, through our website, or from other healthcare providers with the patient's consent.

Use of Personal and Health Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • ✓To provide physiotherapy services and care
  • ✓To communicate with patients regarding their treatment and care
  • ✓To process payments and manage billing
  • ✓To comply with regulatory requirements and laws
  • ✓To improve our services and quality of care

Disclosure of Personal and Health Information

We may disclose personal and health information to:

  • ✓Other healthcare providers involved in the patient's care
  • ✓Insurance companies and billing agencies
  • ✓Regulatory bodies and authorities as required by law
  • ✓Our employees and contractors who require access to perform their duties

We will obtain the patient's consent before disclosing their personal and health information, except where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Security and Protection of Personal and Health Information

We take reasonable steps to protect personal and health information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction. This includes:

  • ✓Implementing physical, technical, and administrative safeguards
  • ✓Limiting access to authorized personnel
  • ✓Using secure communication methods, such as encrypted email
  • ✓Regularly reviewing and updating our security measures

Access to Personal and Health Information

Patients have the right to access their personal and health information. To request access, patients must submit a written request to our office. We will respond to the request within a reasonable timeframe and may require verification of the patient's identity.

Correction of Personal and Health Information

Patients have the right to request corrections to their personal and health information. To request a correction, patients must submit a written request to our office, specifying the information to be corrected and the reason for the correction. We will respond to the request within a reasonable timeframe.

Complaints and Concerns

If a patient has a complaint or concern regarding our handling of their personal and health information, they may submit a written complaint to our office. We will investigate the complaint and respond to the patient within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on our website and made available at our office. We encourage patients to review this policy regularly to stay informed about our practices and procedures.
