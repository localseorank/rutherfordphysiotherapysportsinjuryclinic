Acupuncture Basics

Acupuncture is a form of traditional Chinese medicine that involves the insertion of fine needles into specific points on the body. It's based on the idea that the body has a flow of energy, or qi, that can become blocked or imbalanced, leading to pain and other health problems. By stimulating specific points along the body's energy pathways, acupuncture can help restore balance and promote healing. Our physiotherapists at Rutherford Physiotherapy & Sports Injury Clinic are trained to use acupuncture in conjunction with other physiotherapy techniques, such as manual therapy and exercise therapy, to provide comprehensive pain management.

When it comes to managing pain, acupuncture can be a highly effective treatment option. It's particularly useful for patients who have tried other forms of treatment without success, or for those who are looking for a more holistic approach to pain management. Our team of physiotherapists will work with you to determine if acupuncture is right for you, and will develop a treatment plan that incorporates acupuncture and other therapies to achieve the best possible outcomes.

Acupuncture is a relatively painless procedure, and most patients find it to be a relaxing and calming experience. The needles used are extremely fine, and are inserted just beneath the skin's surface. Some patients may feel a mild sensation, such as a tingling or warmth, as the needle is inserted, but this is usually mild and temporary.

In addition to its use in pain management, acupuncture can also be used to treat a range of other health conditions, including migraines, arthritis, and digestive problems.
