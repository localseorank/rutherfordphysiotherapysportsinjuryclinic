Frequently Asked Questions

What is physiotherapy and how can it help me?

Physiotherapy is a healthcare profession that focuses on helping individuals recover from injury or illness, and can help with pain management, mobility, and overall physical function.

How do I find a good physiotherapist in Vaughan?

You can find a good physiotherapist in Vaughan by asking for referrals from your doctor or friends, checking online reviews, and verifying their credentials and experience.

What conditions can physiotherapy in Vaughan help with?

Physiotherapy in Vaughan can help with a wide range of conditions, including sports injuries, chronic pain, arthritis, stroke rehabilitation, and post-surgical recovery.

Do I need a doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist in Vaughan?

No, you do not need a doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist in Vaughan, but some insurance companies may require one for reimbursement.

What can I expect during my first physiotherapy session in Vaughan?

During your first physiotherapy session in Vaughan, you can expect a thorough assessment of your condition, a discussion of your goals and treatment options, and the development of a personalized treatment plan.

How long does a typical physiotherapy session in Vaughan last?

A typical physiotherapy session in Vaughan can last anywhere from 30 to 60 minutes, depending on the individual's needs and the type of treatment being provided.

Will my insurance cover physiotherapy services in Vaughan?

Many insurance plans cover physiotherapy services in Vaughan, but coverage and requirements can vary depending on the insurance provider and the individual's policy.

Can I do physiotherapy exercises at home in Vaughan?

Yes, your physiotherapist in Vaughan can provide you with exercises and stretches to do at home to help you recover and maintain your physical function, and can also provide guidance on how to modify your home environment to promote safety and mobility.
