Physiotherapy in Vaughan can help with a wide range of conditions, including sports injuries, chronic pain, arthritis, stroke rehabilitation, and post-surgical recovery.

Will my insurance cover physiotherapy services in Vaughan?

Many insurance plans cover physiotherapy services in Vaughan, but coverage and requirements can vary depending on the insurance provider and the individual's policy.

Can I do physiotherapy exercises at home in Vaughan?

Yes, your physiotherapist in Vaughan can provide you with exercises and stretches to do at home to help you recover and maintain your physical function, and can also provide guidance on how to modify your home environment to promote safety and mobility.
