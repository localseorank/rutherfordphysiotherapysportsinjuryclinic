This traditional Chinese medicine technique has been used for centuries to treat a range of conditions, from musculoskeletal injuries to chronic pain syndromes. At our Vaughan Physiotherapy Clinic, we've seen firsthand the benefits of acupuncture in conjunction with other physiotherapy services.
